# Executive Assistant Roadmap

## Now

- Create executive briefs from internal state.
- Convert brief follow-ups into internal task proposals.
- Create internal email draft proposals from ingested mailbox messages.
- Keep all external send/schedule actions disabled.

## Next

- Add email draft review lifecycle.
- Create approval requests for approved email drafts.
- Add read-only real email connector after explicit approval.

## Later

- Calendar event ingestion.
- Calendar prep briefs.
- Approval-backed email send adapter.
- Approval-backed calendar scheduling adapter.
