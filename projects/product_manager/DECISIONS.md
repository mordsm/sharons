# Product Manager Decisions

## Active Decisions

- Product Manager lives in the `product` Django app.
- Product work starts as contracts and internal drafts.
- Approved drafts can propose internal tasks. They do not write to a product repository.
- A milestone may be linked only to an active objective that is already linked to the same project.
- User research and competitive analysis stay internal. They cannot store a direct contact channel.
- A pricing experiment brief is an internal ILS comparison on a read-only spec. It records two to four proposed amounts and a review decision. It does not charge, spend, publish, or contact a customer.
- GitHub access is one action, `create_issue`, and it runs only after approval of that exact payload.

## Deferred Decisions

- Beta-feedback import from a live customer channel.
