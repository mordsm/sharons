# Chief of Staff Decisions

## Active Decisions

- Chief of Staff is implemented across `core`, `operations`, `portfolio` and `governance`.
- CoS can propose internal work, but cannot execute external actions.
- All state-changing APIs require idempotency and correlation IDs.
- Task completion requires acceptance criteria and verified evidence.

## Deferred Decisions

- Whether to promote CoS into a dedicated Django app.
- Exact objective health scoring formula.
- Whether weekly review should create persistent report artifacts.
